# Historical exploratory material

Earlier notebooks, drafts, review documents, loose exploratory plots, and
notebook editing utilities are retained here for provenance. They are not the
active scientific pipeline and are not represented as completed collaborator
deliverables. Files were moved without altering their contents.

Notebook and utility relative paths may reflect their original root location.
Do not run a historical notebook or patch utility against active files without
reviewing its paths and assumptions. The previous released manuscript and the
data paths needed by its pipeline remain at their original locations.
