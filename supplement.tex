Ayan Mitra and Vasilios Zarikas
\end{center}
This catalogue accompanies the seasonal SURD analysis. Tables S1--S2 give common-lag unique self-history maxima, separated from the cross-variable results in the main paper. Tables S3--S14 give the three leading named cross-unique, redundant and synergistic components for every unequal-lag case. Each entry lists $a:f_a/g_a/\ell\;(L)$ at that atom's own maximum $g_a=I_a/H(Y)=(1-\ell)f_a$. Peaks at different lags must not be added. The base lag $L$ is in observed days; the shifted wing is sampled at $L+5$ days. F5100 and total sets retain the notation $(F,B,C,R)$ and $(H,B,C,R)$. All values are recalculated from the archived CSVs; full-precision values and alternative $f_a$ maxima are supplied with the source package.
