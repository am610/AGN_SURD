future continuum; complete retained line target curves are unavailable under
the chosen range and guard. The guard is a numerical rule, not evidence of
statistical adequacy. Bin sensitivity remains large. Ordering changes 11 of
240 configurations, while total information and leakage stay invariant to
numerical precision. No reliable physical delay or causal detection is claimed.

Equation 3.18 predictor construction is implemented and tested, and its support
audit is available. Expanded scientific runs are unperformed. Individual
unequal lags are not equivalent to its expanded predictor vector. Initial
categorical calibration is complete, but realistic observing dates, temporal
dependence, interpolation, flux errors, weak signals, and significance across
lag scans have not been calibrated. Exact profile integration reconstruction
remains unresolved; the active inputs use published corrected columns.

Thus the bounded review package is complete, while the full scientific program
in the collaborator specification is not complete.
